# Evidence Library: Authoritative Indoor Air Quality & Public Health

All deterministic rules and recommendations in GharKaGod cite real, peer-reviewed public health guidelines. Below is the curated reference library used by the rule engine.

## Reference Index

### [WHO-2021] World Health Organization
- **Title:** WHO Global Air Quality Guidelines: Particulate Matter ($PM_{2.5}$ and $PM_{10}$), Ozone, Nitrogen Dioxide, Sulfur Dioxide and Carbon Monoxide.
- **Year:** 2021.
- **Key Findings:** Annual mean $PM_{2.5}$ should not exceed $5\,\mu\text{g/m}^3$; 24-hour mean should not exceed $15\,\mu\text{g/m}^3$. Exposure to combustion byproducts indoors is a primary driver of acute lower respiratory infections and chronic obstructive pulmonary disease.
- **Link:** https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789240034228

### [WHO-IAQ-2010] World Health Organization
- **Title:** WHO Guidelines for Indoor Air Quality: Selected Pollutants.
- **Year:** 2010.
- **Key Findings:** Specifies exposure limits for benzene, carbon monoxide, formaldehyde, nitrogen dioxide, and polycyclic aromatic hydrocarbons (PAHs) originating from indoor heating, cooking, and building materials.
- **Link:** https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789289002134

### [EPA-COOKING] U.S. Environmental Protection Agency
- **Title:** Indoor Air Quality: Stove and Oven Combustion Byproducts and Kitchen Ventilation.
- **Year:** 2023.
- **Key Findings:** Cooking on gas without range hoods venting outside produces $NO_2$ spikes exceeding ambient standards within minutes. Back burner usage combined with outdoor exhaust reduces indoor exposure by 50% to 70%.
- **Link:** https://www.epa.gov/indoor-air-quality-iaq/indoor-particulate-matter

### [EPA-CLEANING] U.S. EPA & CDC
- **Title:** Volatile Organic Compounds' Impact on Indoor Air Quality.
- **Year:** 2022.
- **Key Findings:** Scented aerosol sprays, bleach mixed with ammoniated cleaners, and synthetic air fresheners release high concentrations of VOCs and secondary organic aerosols. Wet mopping with plain water or diluted mild unscented detergent significantly reduces resuspended particulate matter without VOC emissions.
- **Link:** https://www.epa.gov/indoor-air-quality-iaq/volatile-organic-compounds-impact-indoor-air-quality

### [LANCET-2022] The Lancet Planetary Health
- **Title:** Pollution and Health: A Progress Update (Lancet Commission on Pollution and Health).
- **Year:** 2022.
- **Key Findings:** Indoor air pollution from solid biomass, kerosene, and unvented cooking contributes to over 1.6 million premature deaths annually in South Asia and sub-Saharan Africa. Transitioning to clean fuel, improving ventilation, and behavioral mitigation provide rapid exposure reduction.
- **DOI:** 10.1016/S2542-5196(22)00090-0

### [CPCB-SAMEER] Central Pollution Control Board (India)
- **Title:** National Air Quality Index (NAQI) Standard and Public Health Advisories.
- **Year:** 2015-2024.
- **Key Findings:** Defines Indian AQI categories (Good 0-50, Satisfactory 51-100, Moderate 101-200, Poor 201-300, Very Poor 301-400, Severe 401-500) and dictates advisories on window sealing and outdoor exposure avoidance during severe smog episodes.
- **Link:** https://cpcb.nic.in/national-air-quality-index/

### [CR-BOX-2021] UC Davis / EPA
- **Title:** Efficacy of Do-It-Yourself (DIY) Air Cleaners: The Corsi-Rosenthal Box.
- **Year:** 2021-2023.
- **Key Findings:** A box fan with four MERV-13 filters delivers Clean Air Delivery Rates (CADR) equivalent or superior to commercial HEPA air purifiers at less than 20% of the cost (under ₹3,500 / $45), dramatically lowering indoor fine particulate counts.
- **Link:** https://ehp.niehs.nih.gov/doi/10.1289/EHP9408
